Problem Statements & Solutions

PROBLEM #01

Lack of Awareness

Users find traditional cybersecurity training boring. Static PDFs and videos fail to engage users, leading to poor retention of critical defense concepts.

OUR COUNTER MEASURE

We implemented a Dynamic Quiz Engine with randomized question pools. It provides immediate educational feedback and tracks progress visually to ensure active learning.

PROBLEM #02

Email Spoofing

Attackers use "Typosquatting" (e.g., paypa1.com) and fake urgency. Users struggle to verify sender authenticity manually.

OUR COUNTER MEASURE

We built a Heuristic Email Scanner. It validates domains against known disposable providers and analyzes content for phishing triggers (e.g., "Urgent" + "Bank").

PROBLEM #03

Malicious URLs

3.5 billion phishing URLs are created annually. Visual inspection of links is no longer sufficient to stop credential theft.

OUR COUNTER MEASURE

We developed an Async Terminal Scanner that simulates a robust security tool. It resolves protocols, checks blacklists, and flags suspicious TLDs in real-time.
